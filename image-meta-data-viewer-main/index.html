Before you share, check your photos

Don’t assume a photo is safe to send. A file straight from your camera can reveal exactly when and where it was taken. Check every photo with this tool first, and if it contains location data, use the metadata removal feature before you share it. Sharing without checking can expose where you live, where you go, and what device you use.
